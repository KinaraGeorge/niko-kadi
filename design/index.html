Same cards, same rules

The power cards you grew up with.

Play the way your family does. Switch house rules on or off before the first deal.

  • 2♥

    Pick two

    Next player draws two, or stacks another 2 on top.

  • 3♣

    Pick three

    Draw three, or pass the pain along with another 3.

  • J♠

    Skip

    The next player sits this turn out.

  • K♦

    Kickback

    The order of play reverses.

  • 8♣

    Question

    Answer with a card of the same suit, or draw.

  • A♠

    Change suit

    Call any suit you like. Make them regret that heart.

Rules differ between towns and families, so each table can adjust them.
